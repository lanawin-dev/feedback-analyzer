Customer Satisfaction Survey — Solstice Manufacturing Co
Respondent: Linda Park (IT Systems Administrator)
Date: 2026-09-12

CSAT (1-5): 4

Q1: What's working well for your team right now?
A1: Once we found the bulk user import feature, onboarding new shop-floor
staff became much faster. Support has also been good about following up
rather than letting things go quiet.

Q2: What's the biggest friction point, if any?
A2: Two things: our year-over-year production reports take 15-20 seconds
to load, which people notice but isn't a dealbreaker. Bigger one is
authentication — we're still waiting on centralized login through our
Okta setup, which was flagged as a high-priority item in our last
security review.

Q3: Is there anything you wish the product could do that it can't today?
A3: Single sign-on through our corporate identity provider (Okta) is the
main one, and it's tied to a compliance finding on our end so it's more
than a convenience request for us at this point.

Customer Satisfaction Survey — Brightpath Health Partners
Respondent: Elaine Foster (Senior Program Coordinator)
Date: 2026-09-10

CSAT (1-5): 3

Q1: What's working well for your team right now?
A1: The day-to-day case note workflow still works well for us, and the
support team continues to be kind and responsive whenever we reach out —
that hasn't changed.

Q2: What's the biggest friction point, if any?
A2: I don't want this to sound harsher than it is, but a few of the
things we've flagged over the past couple of months (single sign-on with
our Azure AD, audit log exports, and the permissions screen being
confusing for new staff) are all still unscheduled. Individually each one
is manageable, but together they're starting to add up, especially with
our compliance team asking about the SSO timeline more frequently now.

Q3: Is there anything you wish the product could do that it can't today?
A3: Same items as before, mainly: single sign-on / login through our
existing hospital network account, and a self-serve audit log export for
our compliance reviews. Both have come up in more than one internal
meeting on our side now.

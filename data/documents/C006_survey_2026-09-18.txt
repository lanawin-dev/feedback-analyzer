Customer Satisfaction Survey — Fernwood Media Group
Respondent: Tom Reilly (Head of Operations)
Date: 2026-09-18

CSAT (1-5): 4

Q1: What's working well for your team right now?
A1: The new video review workflow with timestamped comments has been a
genuine win for our editors, and support has been consistently friendly
and clear whenever we reach out.

Q2: What's the biggest friction point, if any?
A2: Password resets — since login isn't tied to our Google Workspace
account, our small IT team handles roughly 8-10 reset tickets a week for
this platform alone. Not a dealbreaker, just an ongoing operational drag.

Q3: Is there anything you wish the product could do that it can't today?
A3: Login through our existing Google Workspace account (or a proper SSO
option) would remove most of the reset ticket volume. Also would love
Slack notifications, or at least email digests, for comment activity
instead of having to check in-app.

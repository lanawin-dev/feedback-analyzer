Customer Satisfaction Survey — Anchor & Vale Consulting
Respondent: Robert Vale (Founder)
Date: 2026-09-05

CSAT (1-5): 4

Q1: What's working well for your team right now?
A1: For a solo consulting operation, the core tool covers what I need —
client notes, scheduling, invoicing all in one place. Support has been
responsive whenever I've run into issues.

Q2: What's the biggest friction point, if any?
A2: The mobile app logging me out randomly when switching networks has
been the most annoying part, and it caused a few calendar sync drops too.
Support says a fix is coming, so I'm hopeful. The itemized invoices are
also more detail than I need most months.

Q3: Is there anything you wish the product could do that it can't today?
A3: A dark mode would be nice since I do a lot of late-night work. Also a
simplified, non-itemized invoice summary would save me a minute or two
each month — not a big deal, just a nice-to-have.

Customer Satisfaction Survey — Cedarline Logistics
Respondent: Priya Nair (IT Manager)
Date: 2026-09-20

CSAT (1-5): 2

Q1: What's working well for your team right now?
A1: When it's up and running, the core dispatch view does what we need.
Support staff individually (Daniel especially) have been responsive and
honest with us, that part I don't want to lose in the rest of this
feedback.

Q2: What's the biggest friction point, if any?
A2: Reliability. We've had three significant outages this quarter during
active shifts, which for a logistics operation means trucks and drivers
sitting idle with no visibility. Combined with a pricing increase earlier
in the year, it's hard to justify internally right now.

Q3: Is there anything you wish the product could do that it can't today?
A3: The warehouse management system integration we requested back in June
is still not live. Our team is manually double-entering shipment data
every day because of it. At this point we are actively looking at other
platforms, mainly because of the combination of downtime and this
integration gap — not because of any one single issue.

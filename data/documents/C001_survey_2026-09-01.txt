Customer Satisfaction Survey — Nimbus Retail Group
Respondent: Mike Chen (Operations Manager)
Date: 2026-09-01

CSAT (1-5): 5

Q1: What's working well for your team right now?
A1: The dashboards have completely changed how our regional managers
engage with the data — they log in on their own now instead of asking me
for screenshots every week. Support has also been consistently fast and
knowledgeable, which matters a lot to us.

Q2: What's the biggest friction point, if any?
A2: Honestly nothing major. If I had to name something, exporting our
biggest quarterly reports (300k+ rows) still feels a little opaque — it
just spins with no progress indicator, and it'd be nice to schedule those
reports to send automatically instead of pulling them by hand every
Monday.

Q3: Is there anything you wish the product could do that it can't today?
A3: Scheduled/recurring report delivery would be the main one — send the
weekly ops report to a distribution list automatically. Beyond that we're
in good shape.

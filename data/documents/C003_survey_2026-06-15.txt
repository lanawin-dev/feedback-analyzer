Customer Satisfaction Survey — Brightpath Health Partners
Respondent: Elaine Foster (Senior Program Coordinator)
Date: 2026-06-15

CSAT (1-5): 5

Q1: What's working well for your team right now?
A1: Overall really positive experience. The case note system has made
coordination between our care teams much smoother, and support has always
been quick to respond when we've had questions.

Q2: What's the biggest friction point, if any?
A2: Nothing major comes to mind. Maybe the permissions/roles screen could
be a little clearer for staff who are less technical, but it's a small
thing.

Q3: Is there anything you wish the product could do that it can't today?
A3: Not really, we're happy with where things stand. If anything, it
would be nice long-term to log in with our hospital network credentials
instead of a separate password, but that's more of a convenience thing
than a real problem right now.
